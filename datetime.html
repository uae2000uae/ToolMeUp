How to use

  1. World clock: add cities with the search box, then drag the slider to find a time that works for everyone. Night hours are shaded.
  2. Countdown: name an event and pick a date. Copy the share link to send a live countdown to someone else.
  3. Age and Date difference work out exact years, months and days, plus totals and working days.
How are business days counted?

Every day between the two dates is counted except weekend days. By default the weekend is Saturday–Sunday (as in the UAE since 2022); switch it to Friday–Saturday if needed. Public holidays aren't excluded.

How accurate is the Hijri date?

It uses your browser's built-in Umm al-Qura calendar. Official dates can differ by a day because they depend on moon sighting.
