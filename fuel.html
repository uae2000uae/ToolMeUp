Why can't mpg be converted by multiplying by a fixed number?

mpg and km/L measure distance per unit of fuel, while L/100 km measures fuel per distance. They are inverses: L/100 km = 235.21 ÷ mpg (US), or 282.48 ÷ mpg (UK).

What is MPGe?

MPGe (miles per gallon equivalent) is the US EPA's figure for electric cars: 33.705 kWh counts as the energy of one gallon of petrol. Canada uses litres-equivalent per 100 km (Le/100 km), where 1 litre of petrol = 8.9 kWh.

What is the "mpg illusion"?

Going from 10 to 20 mpg saves more fuel than going from 30 to 50 mpg, because what you actually save depends on fuel used per distance. That's why the comparison shows litres and cost per 100 km, not just mpg.

Where do the energy and CO₂ figures come from?

We use typical lower heating values: petrol 8.9 kWh/L, diesel 9.9, E85 6.5, LPG 6.8, CNG 13.1 kWh/kg and hydrogen 33.3 kWh/kg. Real values vary a little by blend and country. Enter electric consumption as shown by your car or charger.
