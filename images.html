    How to use

    1. Drop one or more images (or paste a screenshot). They're compressed straight away with the settings on the right.
    2. Tune quality, format and size — every image updates. Click a thumbnail to compare before and after with a slider.
    3. Download images one by one, or all at once as a ZIP.
    Which format should I choose?

    WebP gives the smallest files that every modern browser supports — great for websites. JPG is best for compatibility (email, older apps). PNG keeps sharp edges and transparency but is large for photos. AVIF is even smaller than WebP where supported.

    Why did an image get bigger?

    Already well-compressed images (or PNG screenshots converted to JPG at high quality) can grow. When that happens the original is kept automatically and marked "kept original".
