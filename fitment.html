Baseline setup

The wheels and tires on your car today. Everything else is compared against this.

Wheel

The distance (mm) from the wheel's mounting face to its centerline: a higher number tucks the wheel further inward, a lower or negative number pushes it outward.
A plate (mm) fitted between the hub and wheel that pushes the whole wheel outward. Leave it empty if no spacer fitted.

Tire

The first number is the tire width in (mm); the second is sidewall height as a (%) of that width. The R number is taken from your rim diameter.
Some tire types run wider, this helps us make sure your tire is free from touching anything. Leave it 0 if you don't know yours
Sidewall bulge past the rim edge, shown in the drawing. 5% is typical.

Speedometer

The majority of OEM installations will make speedometer give you faster reading than GPS or Radar speed. This calculator helps provide insight about what the reading is going to be using the new fitment.

As per your settings,
When your Speedometer shows 100 km/h, your actual speed (GPS) is 100 km/h

Fender Clearance

The gap (mm) between the top of your current tire and the wheel arch / fender liner. Taller tires eat into this gap; we use it to warn you if the tire rubs.
Advanced settings (optional)

Vehicle Clearances

This is helpfull to know how much space you have behind the wheel to use for wider wheels, to make sure tires don't touch the inside of the wheel or the outside fender. Providing this with setting the minimums help us warn you when you're too close or more.

Thresholds

These are red-flag thresholds. We will calculate the clearance based on your provided current settings and will warn you if lines are crossed. Calculation will not be blocked.
