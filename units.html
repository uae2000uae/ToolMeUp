Unit Converter

Type a value once and see it in every unit. Click any result to copy it, or click a unit name to convert from it.

How to use

  1. Choose a category: length, weight, temperature, speed, volume, area, pressure, data, fuel economy, power or torque.
  2. Type a value and pick its unit. The main result and the full list below update as you type. Simple math works too, e.g. 5*12 or 3/4.
  3. Click a value in the list to copy it, or click a unit's name to convert from that unit instead.
Why do US and UK gallons give different results?

A US gallon is 3.785 litres, while an imperial (UK) gallon is 4.546 litres. Fuel economy in mpg therefore differs between the two — both are listed.

What's the difference between MB and MiB?

MB (megabyte) uses powers of 1000 (1 MB = 1,000,000 bytes), which is how drive makers count. MiB uses powers of 1024 (1 MiB = 1,048,576 bytes), which is how many operating systems report sizes.
