    How to use

    1. Pick a calculator from the tabs at the top. The tab is remembered, and you can link to one directly, e.g. calculators.html#loan.
    2. Type your numbers — results update instantly. There's no "calculate" button.
    3. Click any result to copy it. Your loan and savings inputs are remembered on this device.
    How is the monthly loan payment calculated?

    With the standard amortization formula: payment = P × r / (1 − (1 + r)−n), where P is the amount, r the monthly rate and n the number of months. Fees and insurance are not included.

    What's the difference between simple and compound interest?

    Simple interest is paid only on the original amount. Compound interest is also paid on interest already earned, so the balance grows faster the more often it compounds.
